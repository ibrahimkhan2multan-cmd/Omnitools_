OmniTools is an all-in-one utility suite that works offline, with no account, no ads and no analytics.

Included tools:
- QR and barcode scanner with link safety checks and local history
- QR code generator for text, links, Wi-Fi and contacts
- Notepad with autosave and search
- Text tools: word counts, case, sort, find and replace, Base64, URL encode, JSON format
- Scientific calculator
- Unit and number-base converter
- Device information
- Secure password and UUID generator

Notes, favorites and scan history stay on your device.
